# Boosts

<p class="ed-lead">Pick any coin, pick a pack, burn $1EDGE from your wallet. It lights up on the board with a rocket in its boost colour and the count on its card, and the top boosts lead the trending strip.</p>

## Packs

| Pack | Price | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| 10 boosts | $10 | 12 hours |
| 50 boosts | $40 | 24 hours |
| 100 boosts | $75 | 48 hours |
| 500 boosts | $300 | 72 hours |

Priced in dollars, paid in $1EDGE at the quote you see (it holds for 2 minutes). Any coin on Solana, Robinhood Chain or Arc can be boosted; the burn always happens on Solana, from your own wallet.

## The burn is real

You sign one burn of $1EDGE from your own wallet. 1EDGE never holds it and never signs for you. The burn is on-chain and public, and the server checks it before your boosts count.

## The rocket

A coin's score is the boosts active on it right now, up to **1,000**. The rocket takes its colour from the score: Bronze (1–99), Silver (100–249), Ice (250–499), Violet (500–999), and Gold at MAX. Boosts past the cap become extra time at MAX, never extra points.

## Where to boost

Menu → **Boosts**, or **Boost this token ›** on a coin's chart.

!!! warning "Boosts buy attention, not quality"
    A boost says someone paid to put a coin in front of you. Nothing more. Check the coin.
