# <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-stock.svg" alt=""> EDGEstocks

<p class="ed-lead">Coins priced in a real, tokenised stock, on Solana and Robinhood Chain. The pink mark. On Robinhood Chain, a disc with the stock's logo next to the chain mark: hover or tap it for <em>Trades in $SYM</em>.</p>

## On Solana

An **open launch on Meteora**, priced in a tokenised stock: xStocks by Backed and Backpack stocks, for example TSLAx.

- On Meteora from the first trade. Pass holders buy until it graduates.
- Buys are paid in the stock, and sells pay out the stock.
- Total fee **3%**: 1EDGE's 1%, a builder fee you route to up to 4 wallets, and LP compound for the rest (at least 0.2%).
- No buyback & burn and no Stack on stock launches for now. Fees are taken in the stock.
- Holders don't earn the stock here.
- Launch fee **$10**.

## On Robinhood Chain

A **1EDGE curve launch priced in a stock**: a Robinhood Stock Token you pick, for example TSLA. Buyers pay in the stock and sellers get the stock back. When the curve fills, the coin graduates to Uniswap v4, paired with the stock.

- Fixed fees, like EDGE: **1.15%**. 1EDGE 0.55% · creator 0.40% · LP 0.20%. Every fee is taken in the stock, and your creator cut is paid in the stock.
- No builder fee, no buyback & burn and no Stack on a stock launch for now.
- Holders don't earn the stock. Holding the coin isn't holding the stock.
- Your dev buy, up to 5% of supply, can be paid in ETH (swapped into the stock inside the launch) or in the stock itself.
- The graduation target is set per stock, in that stock.
- No rebates or referral share on stock launches.
- The stock's issuer can freeze or block a launch's reserve. Nothing 1EDGE controls can move it while frozen.
- Launch fee **$2**, paid in ETH.

## Where you are matters

Stock launches, stock pairs and stock rewards aren't available everywhere. Where they aren't, you'll see *Stock launches aren't available in your country.* See [Regions](../help/regions.md).

!!! warning "Not the stock itself"
    An EDGEstocks coin is a memecoin. Holding it isn't holding the stock, and the stock's price moves the coin's price. Nothing here is investment advice.
