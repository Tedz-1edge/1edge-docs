# <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-stock.svg" alt=""> EDGEstocks

<p class="ed-lead">Coins tied to a real, tokenised stock, on Solana and Robinhood Chain. The pink mark. On Robinhood Chain, a disc with the stock's logo next to the chain mark: hover or tap it for <em>Pays $SYM</em>.</p>

## On Solana

An **open launch on Meteora**, priced in a tokenised stock: xStocks by Backed and Backpack stocks, for example TSLAx.

- On Meteora from the first trade. Pass holders buy until it graduates.
- Buys are paid in the stock, and sells pay out the stock.
- Fixed fees, nothing to set, like EDGE.
- An EDGEtek version lets the creator set the fees: a builder fee (routed to up to 4 wallets), buyback & burn and LP compound. Total fee 2, 3, 4 or 5%. LP compound takes whatever the other shares leave. No Stack.
- Holders don't earn the stock here.
- Launch fee **$10**.

## On Robinhood Chain

### Holders earn a stock


The coin trades in **ETH on the 1EDGE curve**, like any 1EDGE launch. Part of every trade buys the stock the creator picked and pays it to holders, pro-rata.

| Share | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Stock to holders** | Buys the stock on every trade, paid to holders pro-rata. Presets: Light 1%, Standard 3%, Max 3.8%, or custom. |
| **LP compound** | Deepens the pool. |
| **Buyback & burn** | Optional. |
| **Creator** | A fixed 0.2% of volume, paid in the stock, out of 1EDGE's 1% fee. Not added to the tax. |

- The creator's total tax is capped at 4% (the contract's 5% all-in, less 1EDGE's 1%).
- No builder fee on a stock launch.
- Launch fee **$10**. One signature.

### Priced in a stock

An **open launch on Uniswap v4**, priced in the stock itself, on Robinhood Chain.

- On Uniswap from the first trade. Pass holders buy until it graduates. Buyers hold the coin at once. Anyone can sell, any time.
- Total fee 2, 3, 4 or 5%: 1EDGE's fixed 1%, the creator's cut (up to 3.8%, paid in the coin), and buyback & burn for the rest.
- **Every fee is taken in the coin, not in the stock.** No LP compound and no Stack. Holders don't earn the stock here.
- Optional market-hours fee: +0.01% to 1% while the US stock market is closed. It buys back and burns the coin.
- No dev buy on these for now.

Same open-launch protections as any other: [Open launches](open-launches.md).

## Where you are matters

Stock launches, stock pairs and stock rewards aren't available everywhere. Where they aren't, you'll see *Stock launches aren't available in your country.* See [Regions](../help/regions.md).

!!! warning "Not the stock itself"
    An EDGEstocks coin is a memecoin. Holding it isn't holding the stock, and stock rewards depend on the coin's trading volume. Nothing here is investment advice.
