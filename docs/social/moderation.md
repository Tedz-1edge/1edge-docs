# Reporting and moderation

<p class="ed-lead">Report from the <strong>⋯</strong> menu on any post, message or account. A person reads every report.</p>

## What you can report

- Scam, phishing or a wallet drainer
- Impersonating a person, project or 1EDGE
- Harassment, hate or threats
- Spam or engagement farming
- Fabricated call or PnL card
- Illegal content
- Something else

*Read by a person. We never tell the reported account who reported them. Reports that are clearly false count against the reporter.* Up to 10 reports a day.

## What can happen

| Step | What it means | How long |
|---|---|---|
| **Limited reach** | Your posts show to fewer people. Silent: no notice, no appeal. | 1, 3, 7 or 14 days |
| **Restricted** | Posting is paused. You can still read, trade and launch. | 3, 7 or 14 days |
| **Suspended** | Your account is paused on social. | 14, 30 or 365 days |

Repeat breaks within 90 days step up. For scams, the pass can be lost.

A restriction or suspension comes with a message from the official **@1EDGE** account, and you can **appeal once** from the notice in the app.

## The rules

The rules are the **Edge Social Guidelines**: [read them on 1edge.app](https://1edge.app/nft?legal=guidelines). You can also report by email to `abuse@1edge.app`.
