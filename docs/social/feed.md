# EdgeSocial

<p class="ed-lead">The feed lives next to the charts. Post, call, react, DM, and see who's actually trading, on the same account as your wallet.</p>

## Posting

- Up to **280 characters**, with an image, GIF or video.
- `$TICKER` links a coin, `@user` links a person, `#tag` links a hashtag. The first link gets a preview card.
- **Repost** or **Quote** from the post menu. Reply to start a thread.
- To post you need a `@username` and to accept the Terms once. 1EDGE screens posts automatically before they go up; a post that breaks the rules can be refused.

## The feed

- **Global:** ranked by replies, reposts, reactions and live calls.
- **Following:** people you follow, newest first. Never ranked.
- **Communities:** your communities.

## Reactions

Twelve: Love, Like, Dislike, Fire, Moon, Diamond, Rekt, Ape, Laugh, Cry, Eyes, Salute. Hold to react on mobile.

## Calls and PnL

Call a coin to your followers or share your PnL straight into the feed. See [Calls, PnL and share cards](../trade/calls-and-pnl.md).

## Communities

- **Groups** anyone can start, open or by request.
- **Token communities**: one per coin, started by the coin's creator.
- Each has its own feed, live chat, roles and bans.

## Messages

- 1:1 messages start with a request the other side accepts. Group chats too.
- **End-to-end encryption is opt-in, 1:1 only.** Tap **Turn on** in a chat. Your wallet signs once per device. Groups, community rooms, attachments and the 1EDGE support thread aren't encrypted.
