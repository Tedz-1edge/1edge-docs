# Notifications

<p class="ed-lead">One switch per kind of notification covers both the bell and push. Change them in <strong>Settings → Notifications</strong>.</p>

## On by default (bell + push)

- Messages (DM push never includes the text)
- Mentions
- Trophies you unlock
- Tier upgrades ready to claim
- Season payouts, the finale and season nudges

## Bell only, by default

Replies, quotes, reactions, reposts, new followers, community requests.

## Off until you turn it on

- **Calls and buys from a trader:** ring the bell on their profile. Per person.
- **The creator wallet sold:** *$X: the creator wallet sold*, on coins you hold.

## Push on your phone

- **Android / Chrome:** tap **Download the app** and install. Turn on push when asked; the site never asks on its own.
- **iPhone:** open 1edge.app in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Push works from the installed app on iOS 16.4 and up.
- Wallet in-app browsers can't install or get push. Open in Safari or Chrome.
