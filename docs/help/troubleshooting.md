# Troubleshooting

<p class="ed-lead">The exact message helps. Find it below. Still stuck? <a href="../support/">Message support</a> with what you were doing and what you saw.</p>

## Signing in and your pass

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Your 1EDGE Wallet was opened with email only. To mint a pass, link Google, X, Discord or a phone number…* | Link one of those to your 1EDGE Wallet, then try again. |
| *This wallet already holds a pass on …* | You're already in on that chain. |
| *The price moved. Check the new amount and tap Mint again.* | The quote refreshed. Check it and mint. |
| *Cancelled. Nothing was sent.* | You rejected it in your wallet. Nothing happened. |
| *Passes go on sale at the opening. Nothing to pay today — check back then.* | Before the doors open. |
| *Payouts and mints are paused for a few minutes. Nothing was charged. Try again shortly.* | A short pause on our side. |
| *This 1EDGE Wallet has already brought a pass.* | One port per account. See [Bring your pass](../start/port.md). |

## Buying and selling

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Not enough SOL for that trade plus fees* | Add SOL or buy less. On Robinhood Chain / Arc: *…ETH / USDC for that buy plus gas*. |
| *Price moved — raise slippage and retry* | Raise slippage a little in Trade Settings, or try again. |
| *Another buy took this slot — try again in a second* | One buy per slot. Tap again. |
| *Someone bought in this block. One buy per block before graduation — tap BUY again.* | Same, on Robinhood Chain / Arc. |
| *Buys open a few seconds after launch — try again* | The opening grace. Wait a moment. |
| *You need a 1EDGE pass to buy this token on the curve.* | [Get your pass](../start/get-your-pass.md). Selling is always open. |
| *Your 1EDGE pass is too new to buy this token yet…* | The creator set a reputation check. It ends within 5 minutes of launch. |
| *That buy is over the wallet cap right now. Buy less.* | The coin's wallet cap. |
| *⏱ Cooldown — wait Ns before your next buy* | The creator set a trade cooldown. |
| *Your priority fee or Jito tip is over this token's fee cap…* | Lower it, or wait for the cap to end. |
| *That sell is over this token's max per sell. Sell a smaller amount.* | The creator set a max per sell. |
| *This token's sell pace is full this block. Try again in a moment.* | The creator set a sell pace. |
| *No wallet-to-wallet sends until graduation. You can sell any time.* | Open launches lock transfers until graduation. |
| *This token needs you to hold another token first. Its page says which.* | Must-hold rule. |
| *Buys are paused on this token. You can still sell.* | Buys are paused. |
| *Cancelled in wallet* | You rejected it. Nothing happened. |
| *Took too long — try again* | The network was slow. Try again. |
| *Your wallet didn't answer. Check your wallet — the trade may have gone through…* | Check before you try again; it may have landed. |
| *Switch your wallet to Robinhood Chain and retry* | Your own wallet is on the wrong network. |

## Launching

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Not enough SOL in this wallet — short by at least X SOL…* | A launch pays rent for new accounts plus your dev buy and the network fee. Add SOL or lower the dev buy. |
| Your ticker shows **Established**, **Evaluating**, **Cooling** or **Reserved** | Copycat protection. See [Launch a coin](../launch/launching.md#copycat-protection). |

## Wallet, sends and bridging

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *More than you can send. Keep X for the network fee.* | Send a little less. |
| *That is an EVM (0x) address, and this send is on Solana…* | Wrong address type for this chain. |
| *Your wallet is on another network… Nothing was sent.* | Switch networks in your wallet. |
| *Arc to Robinhood is not open yet. Go Arc → Solana → Robinhood.* | Use two hops. |
| *The move did not go through, so your … was returned to your wallet.* | Nothing lost. Try again. |

## Where you are

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *Not available where you are* | 1EDGE isn't available in your country. See [Regions](regions.md). |
| *We couldn't tell where you're connecting from, so this action is paused…* | Turn off any VPN, proxy or Tor and try again. |
