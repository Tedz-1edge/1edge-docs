# Regions

<p class="ed-lead">1EDGE isn't available everywhere. Some features (stocks and Earn) are available in fewer places than the rest. Where you are is checked when you act, not just when you visit.</p>

## What you'll see

- **Where 1EDGE isn't available:** *Not available where you are. Sorry, 1EDGE isn't available in your country, so you can't use the site from here.* Nothing that uses your account works from there: no pass, no trading, no launching, no claiming.
- **The first time you do something:** *One quick thing, asked once:* you confirm where you are.
- **If we can't tell where you are:** *We couldn't tell where you're connecting from, so this action is paused. If you use a VPN, proxy or Tor, turn it off and try again.*
- **Stocks and Earn** are blocked in more places than the rest of 1EDGE. You'll see *Stock launches aren't available in your country* or *Earn isn't available where you are*.
- The card on-ramp is hidden where 1EDGE isn't available.

!!! warning "VPNs"
    *Using a VPN or proxy to get around this isn't allowed, and any rewards earned that way can't be paid out.*
