# Stay safe

<p class="ed-lead">Most losses in crypto aren't hacks. They're people handing over a key or signing something they didn't read. Five rules cover nearly all of it.</p>

## 1. Your key is your wallet

Your 1EDGE Wallet key and any seed phrase control your funds **and your pass**.

- Keep them in a password manager. Never in a screenshot, a note app or a chat.
- **Never share them. 1EDGE will never ask for them**: not support, not Edgar, not an admin, not a "validator".
- Export your key only into apps you trust. If it leaks, move your funds out right away.

## 2. Only sign what you started

**The port-link scam.** Someone DMs you a link: "claim your bonus", "verify your pass", "migrate now". It's a real `1edge.app/port` page, but the ticket points at *their* account. Sign it and your pass goes to them.

The port page warns you every time: *Only sign if you started this yourself, from your own 1EDGE Wallet, in the last 10 minutes. If someone sent you this link, close this page.* It also names the account the pass goes to: ***Your pass goes to the 1EDGE account @… Is that you?*** If it isn't you, close it. See [Bring your pass](../start/port.md).

The same goes for any signature: read the wallet prompt. If you didn't start it, reject it.

## 3. Support doesn't DM you first

Real support is the **@1EDGE** thread in the app and **Edgar** on Telegram. 1EDGE doesn't send unprompted DMs asking you to fix, verify or claim anything. The @1EDGE account does send notices (like a moderation notice or a season recap); it never asks for a key, a payment or a signature. See [Support](support.md).

## 4. Check you're in the right place

- The site is **1edge.app**. Docs are **docs.1edge.app**. Bookmark them.
- The official account carries the **Official 1EDGE account** mark.
- Our socials are on [Official links](links.md). Anything else is not us.

## 5. Check the coin, not the name

Tickers are cheap. Before you buy:

- Check the contract address on the coin's page, not in a chat.
- Read the marks: chain, mode, ring, rules. [What the marks mean](../trade/marks.md).
- Read *What the trades show*: did the creator sell? How many humans hold?

## If something goes wrong

- **Lost funds, a drain, a hack:** message [support](support.md) right away. It goes straight to a person.
- **A scam, a fake account, a drainer:** report it from the **⋯** menu (*Scam, phishing or a wallet drainer*; *Impersonating a person, project or 1EDGE*). See [Reporting](../social/moderation.md).
- **You signed a port link you didn't start:** message support now.

    Memecoins are high-risk. Prices can go to zero. Only trade what you can afford to lose. Read the [Risk Disclaimer](https://1edge.app/nft?legal=disclaimer).
