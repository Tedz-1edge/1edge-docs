# Get your pass

<p class="ed-lead">One pass, earned with a quick human check, is your way in. It's one per person, it lives in your wallet, and it can't be sold or sent.</p>

## What the pass gets you

- **Buying on the curve.** Every coin on 1EDGE fills a bonding curve before it graduates. Only pass holders can buy on it. Selling, and trading after graduation, work for anyone.
- **Launching.** Every launch on 1EDGE needs your pass on that chain.
- **Your tier and your rebates.** Every pass starts at Core: 10% of the 1EDGE fee back on your EDGE trades. See [Tiers & rebates](../rewards/tiers.md).
- **Your referral link**, `1edge.app/r/<you>`. See [Referrals](../rewards/referrals.md).

On the mint page the pass is called your **Proof of Life**. Same thing.

!!! info "Soulbound"
    Your pass is tied to your wallet. It can't be transferred. One wallet, one pass, recorded on-chain.

## How it works

<div class="ed-steps" markdown>

1. **Sign up with the 1EDGE Wallet.** Google, X, Discord or a phone number. The wallet is made for you, no extension, no seed phrase to write down. More on [your wallet](wallet.md).
2. **Take the check.** One short challenge, about a minute: a Cloudflare check, then *"Prove you have a hand"*: drag through every checkpoint in order without lifting off. That's it.
3. **Pay with your coin.** SOL on Solana, ETH on Robinhood Chain or USDC on Arc. The price and the network are shown before you sign. One approval in your wallet.
4. **Claim your name.** Pick your `@username` and your link before anyone else takes them. See [Your profile](profile.md).

</div>

!!! warning "Email alone isn't enough to mint"
    If you signed up with email only, link Google, X, Discord or a phone number to your 1EDGE Wallet first. The site tells you before the check starts.

## One pass, three chains

Pay once and you're in on all three chains: **Solana, Robinhood Chain and Arc**.

- Your Solana pass is minted to your 1EDGE Wallet.
- Your Robinhood Chain and Arc passes **arrive on their own, free**. Your 1EDGE Wallet has one EVM address that works on both, and it's linked to your account right after the mint.
- Paying in ETH or USDC costs the same. 1EDGE mints your Solana pass for you, and the other two follow.

Free passes are once per account, per chain. More on [Chains](chains.md).

## The price

!!! tip "Free mint until 2 Nov 2026"
    Until 2 Nov 2026 the 1EDGE mint fee is free; you only pay the Solana rent. The **(i)** split shows *1EDGE mint fee: free*. From 2 Nov the $2 fee is back.

One price, whichever coin you pay with: the 1EDGE pass fee (**$2**) plus the Solana rent your pass's accounts need. That came to **about $4.30** when we wrote this. It's priced in dollars and turned into your coin when you sign, from the median of three exchanges. ETH and USDC payers add a little gas.

Tap the **(i)** next to the price to see the split: *1EDGE mint fee* and *Solana rent (your pass's accounts, referral record, network fee)*.

### No SOL yet? Buy it by card

If your wallet is short at the pay step, you may see **Buy SOL with card · Apple Pay · Google Pay**. It runs in Privy's secure window; 1EDGE never sees your card. Card buys start at about **$20**, so whatever's left after the mint stays in your wallet for your first trade. It lands in your 1EDGE Wallet, usually within minutes.

!!! note "If available in your region"
    The card button only shows with a 1EDGE Wallet, in a region where 1EDGE is available, and where the card provider serves you.

## If something goes wrong

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| *This wallet already holds a pass on …* | One pass per wallet. You're already in. |
| *Your 1EDGE Wallet was opened with email only…* | Link Google, X, Discord or a phone number, then try again. |
| *Payouts and mints are paused for a few minutes. Nothing was charged.* | A short pause on our side. Try again shortly. |
| *Not available where you are* | 1EDGE isn't available in your country. See [Regions](../help/regions.md). |
