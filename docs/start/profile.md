# Your profile

<p class="ed-lead">You start with a generated name. Pick your own <code>@username</code> and your link before someone else does.</p>

## Username and link

Tap **Pick yours** on your "you're in" card, or go to **Settings → Profile**.

- **@username**: 3 to 20 characters, letters, numbers and underscore.
- **Your link** `1edge.app/r/<link>`: 3 to 24 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. It's your referral link; see [Referrals](../rewards/referrals.md).

First pick is free. You can change your link once every 30 days.

## Picture and banner

Tap your picture or the banner to change it. JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, under 5 MB. Your link unfurls on X and Telegram as your own card, with your picture and banner.

## Link Telegram

**Settings → Profile → Link Telegram** (also on your "you're in" card).

<div class="ed-steps" markdown>

1. Tap **Link Telegram**. It opens Edgar, the 1EDGE bot, in Telegram.
2. Tap **Start**. The link code works once and lasts 10 minutes.
3. Done. Edgar knows your pass and tier when you ask him for help.

</div>

Edgar only learns that you're linked and your username. Unlink any time from the same place. More on Edgar in [Support](../help/support.md).

## Your public profile

Your profile at `1edge.app/@you` shows your stats, your calls, your trophies and your posts. See [Social](../social/feed.md).
