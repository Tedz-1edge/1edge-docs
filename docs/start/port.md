# Bring your pass

<p class="ed-lead">Minted a pass with your own wallet before the relaunch? Move it to your 1EDGE Wallet. It's free: one signature from your old wallet, a message, not a transaction. It needs no SOL.</p>

## Steps

Start at **Already minted? → Bring your pass** on `1edge.app/in` or on the mint page.

<div class="ed-steps" markdown>

1. **Sign in with your 1EDGE Wallet.** That's where your pass is going.
2. **One quick thing:** confirm where you are (asked once), then a short human check.
3. **Open the link in the wallet you minted with:** Phantom, Solflare or Backpack, by button, QR code, or this browser. The link lasts 10 minutes.
4. **Sign once** on `1edge.app/port`. *From:* the wallet you minted with. *To:* your 1EDGE Wallet.
5. **Done.** Your pass lands in your 1EDGE Wallet at the same tier. Your Robinhood Chain and Arc passes follow.

</div>

Your old pass stays in the old wallet as a keepsake. It no longer unlocks trading.

## Read the warning before you sign

The port page shows this first, every time:

!!! danger "Only sign if you started this yourself"
    *Only sign if you started this yourself, from your own 1EDGE Wallet, in the last 10 minutes. If someone sent you this link, close this page: signing gives your pass to them.*

Under it you see where the pass is going: ***Your pass goes to the 1EDGE account @you. Is that you?*** If that isn't your `@username`, stop.

A scammer can make a port link that points at *their* account and send it to you as "claim your bonus" or "verify your pass". Signing it hands them your pass. 1EDGE never DMs you a port link. See [Stay safe](../help/safety.md).

## Rules

- One port per old wallet, and one per 1EDGE account (*This 1EDGE Wallet has already brought a pass.*).
- Only wallets that held a pass before the relaunch can port.
- Your old referral link doesn't come across; pick a new one. Your past referral credit does.
- Lost the old wallet? Ask [support](../help/support.md).
