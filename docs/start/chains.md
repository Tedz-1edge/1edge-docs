The chain mark sits **top-left** on every coin's picture, so you always know where it trades. Full legend: [What the marks mean](../trade/marks.md).

## At a glance

| | Solana | Robinhood Chain | Arc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coin you pay in | SOL | ETH | USDC |
| Your address | Solana address | your `0x…` address | the same `0x…` address |
| Graduates to | Meteora | Uniswap v4 | Uniswap v4 |
| Graduation target (1EDGE curve) | 85 SOL | 4 ETH | 7,500 USDC |
| Your pass | minted when you pay | arrives free | arrives free |

Graduation targets are read live from the chain; the numbers above are the launch settings.

## Your pass on each chain

Mint once on any chain and you get all three. If you paid on Solana, your Robinhood Chain and Arc passes are minted to your 1EDGE Wallet's EVM address for free, usually right after your mint. Free passes are once per account, per chain.

## Names are per chain

A ticker held on Solana can still be launched on Robinhood Chain, and the other way round. Copycat protection holds names, tickers and images per chain. See [Launching](../launch/launching.md#copycat-protection).

## Moving value between chains

Want a Robinhood Chain coin but only hold SOL? Pay with SOL and it's bridged for you in the same flow. See [Bridging](../trade/bridging.md).
