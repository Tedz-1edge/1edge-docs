### Seasons payouts

The [Season](../rewards/seasons.md) pays its seats in $1EDGE.

- Each seat is worth a **dollar amount**, turned into $1EDGE at the **average price over the 7 days before each Friday lock**.
- The weekly pot is set from $1EDGE's market cap, on a ladder: a bigger market cap moves the pot up a rung. The finale pays double.
- Up to 500 paid seats a week, in four bands. You need 150 points, 1 SOL of net trading (or the same on Robinhood Chain or Arc) and your pass, opted in.
- Seats are paid from the **Seasons rewards wallet** `Ct187uCepjVYrtAwTVAT8Fa7WtxgAnD6wMGfwwHFmxwZ`. The tokens go straight to your wallet, no gas. Claim within 28 days; unclaimed seats roll back.

You can see this week's pot, in dollars and in $1EDGE, on the Seasons page.

### Boosts

Boosts put a coin in front of more people. Pick any coin, pick a pack, and **burn $1EDGE from your wallet**. The coin lights up on the board with a rocket and its boost count, and the top boosts lead the trending strip.

| Pack | Price | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| 10 boosts | $10 | 12 hours |
| 50 boosts | $40 | 24 hours |
| 100 boosts | $75 | 48 hours |
| 500 boosts | $300 | 72 hours |

Priced in dollars, paid in $1EDGE at the price shown when you confirm (the quote holds for 2 minutes). The $1EDGE is burned: it leaves the supply for good. Boosts work on coins from every chain; you burn $1EDGE on Solana. Open the rocket on any coin's page, or **Boosts** in the menu.

A boost is promotion, not a signal. It says someone paid to show the coin, nothing about the coin itself.

### Back $1EDGE

Creators launching on Solana can send a slice of **their own fee** to buying $1EDGE. Switch on **Back $1EDGE** on the launch page and pick the slice:

| Mode | Slice of volume | Comes out of |
|---|---|---|
| EDGEtek | 0.1% to 1%, never more than your builder fee | Your builder fee. It takes 1 of your 4 builder-fee wallets |
| EDGE | 0.1% to 0.4% | Your 0.4% creator cut, sent when you claim your fees (**Claim & back**) |

- Once a coin's slice reaches **1 SOL**, the 1EDGE back wallet buys $1EDGE on its Meteora pool and **burns every token it bought**. Each buy and burn is on-chain and public.
- Traders pay the same fee either way: the slice comes out of the creator's cut, not on top of it.
- The choice is recorded at launch and shown on the coin. Its **Backs $1EDGE** mark appears once the first $1EDGE buy has landed, never before.
- Solana launches only, for now. Back wallet: `5Verzu25DfvVt6inukjJZe8RWjqoUbVSoK3vhuuuQSkG`.

### Partners and onboarding

The team buys $1EDGE on the open market into one public wallet, `BMHppDqrzVmw4XdNdoqcASxZfVbTT8NZUpDdXeFa2fEX`, to pay KOL partners, supply exchange listings and onboard new users. **That wallet never sells.** Every buy and transfer is public; the log is on [Contracts](../help/contracts.md#marketing-onboarding-wallet).

## What $1EDGE doesn't do

So nobody has to guess:

- **Your tier doesn't depend on $1EDGE.** Tiers come from your trading volume or your referrals. Holding $1EDGE doesn't change your [rebate](../rewards/tiers.md).
- **Fees aren't paid in $1EDGE.** Trading fees, launch fees and the pass are paid in the chain's coin: SOL, ETH or USDC. See [Fees](../trade/fees.md).
- **Rebates and referral earnings aren't paid in $1EDGE.** They're paid in the chain's coin.
- **You don't need $1EDGE to trade, launch or play the Season.** You need your [pass](../start/get-your-pass.md).
- Nothing here is a promise about $1EDGE's price.
