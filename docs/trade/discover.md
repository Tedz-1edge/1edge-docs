# Find coins, follow traders

<p class="ed-lead">The Discover board is the home page. Every coin launched on 1EDGE, on every chain, with the marks that tell you what's going on.</p>

## The board

- **Sorts:** Discover, Flow, Heat, New, Movers, Market cap, Last trade.
- **Mode:** All, EDGE or TEK.
- **Stages:** Launching · Pulse · On a dip · The climb (40–79% bonded) · Final stretch (80%+, closest to graduation) · Graduated.
- **Filters:** age, holders, market cap, 24h volume, and traits (Burn, Stack, Human-gated). Save your filter set.
- **Chain:** on desktop, switch the board between Solana, Robinhood Chain, Arc or all of them from the top bar.

Every card carries the marks: [What the marks mean](marks.md).

## Search

**Search tokens, people.** Find any 1EDGE coin by ticker, name, pool or contract address, and any person, community, tag or post.

## Watchlist

Star a coin anywhere. Your Dashboard tracks it: price, market cap, bonding progress. Private to you.

## Follow traders

Tap **Follow** on anyone's profile. Their posts land in your **Following** feed, newest first.

Want more? Ring their **bell**: *Get notified when this account buys or makes a call.* Off by default, per person. Buys from people you follow come batched, about hourly (*3 people you follow bought $X*).

## Lock tokens

From your Dashboard → **Token Locks**, you can lock any token you hold until a date you pick. *No fees: your wallet pays only network rent, under 0.004 SOL.*

- Solana, powered by Jupiter Lock. Some Token-2022 coins lock on Streamflow instead.
- Minimum 10 minutes, default 30 days, no maximum.
- A lock can't be cancelled or redirected. You claim when it unlocks.
- Anyone can lock, not just the creator. Open launches can be locked after graduation.

## Install the app

**Add 1EDGE to your home screen.** It runs full screen, like an app, and gets push notifications. On iPhone: open 1edge.app in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen (iOS 16.4+ for push). See [Notifications](../social/notifications.md).
