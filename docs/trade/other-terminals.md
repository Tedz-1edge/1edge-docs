# Trading from other terminals

<p class="ed-lead">Your 1EDGE Wallet is a normal wallet. Export the key, import it into Axiom, GMGN, Fomo or anything else, and trade from there.</p>

## Use your wallet on a terminal

Open your wallet → **Use this wallet on Axiom · GMGN · Fomo**. You sign in again, tick that you understand, and the key opens in Privy's secure window. Import it into the terminal.

!!! danger "Only into terminals you trust"
    Anyone with this key controls your wallet and your pass. Never paste it on a site you don't trust. 1EDGE will never ask for it.

## What works where

| | On 1edge.app | On another terminal |
|---|---|---|
| **1EDGE launch, before graduation** | ✓ | ✗ Trades only on 1edge.app |
| **Open launch, before graduation: buy** | ✓ | ✓ if the terminal supports Meteora hook pools, from your pass wallet |
| **Open launch, before graduation: sell** | ✓ | ✓ anywhere |
| **Any coin after graduation** | ✓ | ✓ anywhere |

Buying an open launch on its curve needs your **pass wallet**, wherever you buy. The pool checks it. On a Solana open launch's page you'll see where else it trades (*Pass holders buy here or on GMGN*).

## What counts

**Your trades count wherever you make them.** Volume for your [tier](../rewards/tiers.md), your rebates, your referrer's share and [Seasons](../rewards/seasons.md) trading points come from your on-chain trades in 1EDGE coins, made from your 1EDGE wallet. It doesn't matter which app sent them.

Only trades in **1EDGE coins** count: coins launched on 1EDGE, before and after graduation.

**"Being here" only counts on 1edge.app.** It's the Seasons bonus for days you actually used 1EDGE yourself. Time on Axiom doesn't add to it. See [Seasons](../rewards/seasons.md#being-here).
