# Fees

<p class="ed-lead">Every fee, in one place. Trading fees are the same on buys and sells, and they're set on-chain at launch.</p>

## Trading fees

### 1EDGE launches (our curve)

| Mode | On the curve | Split |
|---|---|---|
| <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-edge.svg" alt=""> **EDGE** | **1.15%** | 1EDGE 0.55% · creator 0.40% · LP 0.20% |
| <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-tek.svg" alt=""> **EDGEtek** | **up to 5%** | 1EDGE 1% · builder fee (up to 3.8%) · LP compound (min 0.2%) · buyback & burn · Stack. The creator sets the split; it's on the coin's page. The launch page only offers the shares that run on that chain and venue |
| <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-stock.svg" alt=""> **EDGEstocks**, Robinhood Chain (priced in a stock) | **1.15%** | 1EDGE 0.55% · creator 0.40% · LP 0.20%, all taken in the stock |

EDGE and EDGEtek are the same on Solana, Robinhood Chain and Arc. EDGEstocks on our curve is Robinhood Chain only.

### Open launches

| Mode | Fee | Split |
|---|---|---|
| **Open EDGE, Solana** | **1.2%** | creator 0.40% · 1EDGE 0.56% · Meteora 0.24% |
| **Open EDGE, Robinhood Chain / Arc** | **1.2%** | 1EDGE 0.60% · creator 0.40% · LP 0.20% |
| **Open EDGEtek** | **2, 3, 4 or 5%** | 1EDGE 1% · builder fee up to 3.8% · LP compound the rest (min 0.2%). Robinhood Chain and Arc also offer burn / Stack; Solana doesn't yet |
| **Open EDGEstocks, Solana** (priced in a tokenised stock) | **3%** | 1EDGE 1% · builder fee to up to 4 wallets · LP compound the rest (min 0.2%). Taken in the stock |

Open launches charge the same fee before and after graduation.

### After graduation (1EDGE launches)

| Chain | Pool fee |
|---|---|
| **Solana, EDGE** | Starts at 1.15% and steps down toward **0.55%** as the market cap grows. Meteora keeps 20% of pool fees. |
| **Solana, Tek** | The creator's total, flat. Each share earns 80% of its rate (Meteora keeps 20%). |
| **Robinhood Chain / Arc** | The coin's curve fee, flat, on Uniswap v4. |

### The launch shield

Open launches only. For the first 15 seconds every buy and sell pays a higher fee: **75%**, falling every second to the coin's normal fee. The trade ticket shows the live number. The extra goes to 1EDGE. On Solana, half of 1EDGE's share of shield fees goes into a pot for the wallets that bought after the shield and still hold. It pays out at graduation or 24 hours after the shield, whichever comes first. Coins paired with a stock have no pot, and the pot isn't on Robinhood Chain or Arc yet. See [Open launches](../launch/open-launches.md#launch-shield).

## Your rebate

On EDGE coins, part of the 1EDGE share of the fee you pay comes back to you, by tier: **Core 10% · Alpha 15% · Elite 20% · Apex 25%**. Your referrer gets 25% of the 1EDGE share of those trades. EDGEtek and EDGEstocks coins pay no rebate and no referral share. Neither costs you extra. See [Tiers & rebates](../rewards/tiers.md).

Priced in dollars, paid in the chain's coin. Solana launches also pay the rent for the coin's accounts.

## The pass

One price on every chain: the **$2** pass fee plus the Solana rent your pass needs, about $4.15 in total. Until 2 Nov 2026 the pass fee is free, so you pay only the rent, about $2.15. ETH and USDC payers add gas. See [Get your pass](../start/get-your-pass.md).

See [Bridging](bridging.md).

## Swaps in your wallet

| | 1EDGE fee | Also |
|---|---|---|
| **Swap** in your 1EDGE Wallet (Solana) | **0.25%** | Jupiter's route; the network fee |

The swap sheet shows the 1EDGE fee before you confirm: the same 0.25%, taken in SOL, USDC or USDT. Pairs where we can't take it show no fee line and pay none.

## No fee

- Sending coins and tokens from your 1EDGE Wallet (you pay the network fee).
- Locking tokens (you pay the network rent, under 0.004 SOL).
- Graduation. There's no graduation fee.
- Bringing your old pass across.
