# Fees

<p class="ed-lead">Every fee, in one place. Trading fees are the same on buys and sells, and they're set on-chain at launch.</p>

## Trading fees

### 1EDGE launches (our curve)

| Mode | On the curve | Split |
|---|---|---|
| <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-edge.svg" alt=""> **EDGE** | **1.15%** | 1EDGE 0.55% · creator 0.40% · LP 0.20% |
| <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-tek.svg" alt=""> **EDGEtek** | **up to 5%** | 1EDGE 1% · builder fee (up to 3.8%) · LP compound (min 0.2%) · buyback & burn · Stack. The creator sets the split; it's on the coin's page |
| <img class="mark" src="../../assets/marks/mode-stock.svg" alt=""> **EDGEstocks** (holders earn a stock) | 1EDGE's 1% + the creator's tax (up to 4%) | Stock to holders · LP · burn. The creator's 0.2% comes out of 1EDGE's 1% |

Same on Solana, Robinhood Chain and Arc.

### Open launches

| Mode | Fee | Split |
|---|---|---|
| **Open EDGE, Solana** | **1.2%** | creator 0.40% · 1EDGE 0.56% · Meteora 0.24% |
| **Open EDGE, Robinhood Chain / Arc** | **1.2%** | 1EDGE 0.60% · creator 0.40% · LP 0.20% |
| **Open EDGEtek** | **2, 3, 4 or 5%** | 1EDGE 1% · builder fee up to 3.8% · LP compound the rest (min 0.2%) · burn / Stack |
| **Open EDGEstocks, Robinhood Chain** | **2, 3, 4 or 5%** | 1EDGE 1% · creator cut up to 3.8% · buyback & burn. Taken in the coin |

Open launches charge the same fee before and after graduation.

### After graduation (1EDGE launches)

| Chain | Pool fee |
|---|---|
| **Solana, EDGE** | Starts at 1.15% and steps down toward **0.55%** as the market cap grows. Meteora keeps 20% of pool fees. |
| **Solana, Tek** | The creator's total, flat. Each share earns 80% of its rate (Meteora keeps 20%). |
| **Robinhood Chain / Arc** | The coin's curve fee, flat, on Uniswap v4. |

### The launch shield

Open launches only. For the first 15 seconds every buy and sell pays a higher fee: **75%**, falling every second to the coin's normal fee. The trade ticket shows the live number. The extra goes to 1EDGE. Half of 1EDGE's share of shield fees goes into a pot for the people who bought after the shield and still hold. See [Open launches](../launch/open-launches.md#launch-shield).

## Your rebate

Part of the 1EDGE share of every fee you pay comes back to you, by tier: **Core 10% · Alpha 15% · Elite 20% · Apex 25%**. Your referrer gets 25% of the 1EDGE share of your trades. Neither costs you extra. See [Tiers & rebates](../rewards/tiers.md).

## The pass

One price on every chain: the **$2** pass fee plus the Solana rent your pass needs, about $4.30 in total when we wrote this. ETH and USDC payers add gas. See [Get your pass](../start/get-your-pass.md).

## Moving between chains

| | 1EDGE fee | Also |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay with SOL / ETH / USDC** on a coin's page | **0.25%** | Relay's own cost. $2 min, $2,000 max per move |
| **Bridge** in your wallet | **0%** | Relay's own cost |

See [Bridging](bridging.md).

## No fee

- Sending coins and tokens from your 1EDGE Wallet (you pay the network fee).
- Locking tokens (you pay the network rent, under 0.004 SOL).
- Graduation. There's no graduation fee.
- Bringing your old pass across.
