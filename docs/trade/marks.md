# What the marks mean

<p class="ed-lead">Every coin's picture carries the same small set of marks. Learn them once and you can read the board at a glance: which chain, which mode, where it trades, and what's going on.</p>

### The venue ring

The ring around the picture tells you **where the coin trades right now**.

<div class="ed-legend">
<div><span class="ic"><img src="../../assets/brand/favicon.png" alt="" style="width:20px;height:20px;border-radius:4px"></span><b>Grey ring</b><span class="d"><em>1EDGE launch: trades on 1edge.app.</em> On our curve, not graduated yet.</span></div>
<div><span class="ic"><img src="../../assets/marks/meteora-logo.svg" alt=""></span><b>Meteora ring</b><span class="d">Gold to purple. A Solana open launch, or a Solana coin that graduated. Trades on Meteora.</span></div>
<div><span class="ic"><img src="../../assets/marks/uniswap-logo.svg" alt=""></span><b>Uniswap ring</b><span class="d">Pink. A Robinhood Chain or Arc open launch, or one that graduated. Trades on Uniswap.</span></div>
</div>

For its first hour after graduation, the ring turns gold.

Open launches don't have a mark of their own: the ring is how you spot them. Hover the ring on the coin's page for *Open launch: trades on Meteora* or *Graduated: trades on Uniswap*.

## On the coin's page

<figure class="ed-shot ed-phone"><img src="../../assets/shots/token-open-390.webp" alt="A Solana open launch priced in USDC: chain mark, USDC disc, Meteora ring, mode mark, the shield pill under the name, the bonding bar. Sample data." loading="lazy" width="390"><figcaption>A Solana open launch priced in USDC: chain mark, USDC disc, Meteora ring, mode mark, the shield pill under the name, the bonding bar. Sample data.</figcaption></figure>

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **The shield pill** under the name | Tap it for *How this launch is protected*: every rule on this coin. Dimmed if there are no extra rules. |
| **42% fee · 9s** | The [launch shield](../launch/open-launches.md#launch-shield) is running. Fee now, time left. |
| **3.5% wallet cap** | No wallet can buy past 3.5% of supply while the curve fills. Lifts at graduation. |
| **30s cooldown** | One buy and one sell per wallet every 30 seconds while the curve fills. |
| **1 buy / block** | Open launches: one buy per block goes through. Sells aren't limited by this. |
| **The bonding bar** | How full the curve is. See [Graduation](../launch/graduation.md). |
| **What the trades show** | *No buy on the curve has been sandwiched (N checked)* · *N humans bought · M holding* · *The creator wallet hasn't sold* (or how much it sold). |
| `DEV` | The creator's wallet, in trades and holders. |
| `T10` | A top-10 holder. |
| `YOU` | You. |

After graduation the pill reads *Rules ended at graduation. Anyone can buy, sell and send $SYM now.*

## Marks on people

Next to a `@username`:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Silver hex | *Connected account*: one X or Discord linked. |
| Violet ring | *Two accounts linked.* |
| Official | *Official 1EDGE account.* The only account that speaks for 1EDGE. |
| Crown | Season champion (gold), runner-up or third, worn through the next season. *Daily Crown*: yesterday's #1 trader. |
