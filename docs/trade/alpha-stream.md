Up to five coins per lane. It refreshes every two minutes. The same marks show on the coin's card on the board.

## What counts

**Talking** means a coin's `$TICKER` is being mentioned well above its normal level: at least 5 mentions in 24 hours from at least 5 different wallets, and at least twice its usual daily rate. One person can't carry it: each author counts at most 3 times. Mentions come from posts, calls, PnL shares and community posts.

**Moving** means at least a 10% move in 24 hours, at least 3 trades and real volume, in the top 15% of movers.

## Never paid for

The stream is computed from what people post and what the market does. Nobody can buy a spot on it.

## Light it up

It's your posts that feed it. Mention a coin with `$TICKER` in a post or a call. When it's quiet you'll see *Quiet right now. Mention a token with $TICKER to light it up.*

Groups running the 1EDGE bot get a post when one of their coins enters a lane. See [Telegram bot](../social/telegram-bot.md).
