# Bridging

<p class="ed-lead">Hold SOL, want a Robinhood Chain coin? You don't need to leave 1EDGE. Two ways, both powered by <a href="https://relay.link">Relay</a>, both into your own wallets.</p>

## Pay with another coin

On a coin's page, if you don't hold that chain's coin, switch to **Pay with SOL** (or ETH, or USDC).

<div class="ed-steps" markdown>

1. Pick the amount. You see the estimate: *≈ X arrives · moving costs ≈ $Y, incl. 1EDGE fee*.
2. Approve once. Your coin moves to **your own wallet** on the coin's chain, usually in about a second.
3. The buy runs as normal.

</div>

- **1EDGE fee: 0.25%** of the amount moved, on top of Relay's own cost. Then the normal trading fee.
- **$2 minimum, $2,000 maximum** per move.
- If the move doesn't go through, your coin is returned to your wallet.
- If the buy finishes the curve, it only takes what was needed; the rest stays in your wallet on that chain.

## Bridge

Open your wallet → **Cross-chain · Bridge**. *Move your money between Solana, Robinhood Chain and Arc. Your own wallets, about a second.*

- **1EDGE fee: 0%.** You pay Relay's cost to move; it's shown before you approve, with what you'll receive.
- Wallet to wallet, your own only.

## Buying SOL by card

At the mint step you may be able to buy SOL by card or Apple Pay / Google Pay, from about $20, *if available in your region*. See [Get your pass](../start/get-your-pass.md#no-sol-yet-buy-it-by-card).
