# Calls, PnL and share cards

<p class="ed-lead">Put your name on a coin. A call is recorded at the market cap of the moment and judged later. You can't edit it.</p>

## Make a call

On a coin's page, tap **CALL**: *Call this token to your followers*.

<div class="ed-steps" markdown>

1. You see *RECORDING AT $X*: the market cap now, set by 1EDGE from the latest trade.
2. Add *Why?* if you want, up to 280 characters.
3. **CALL IT.** Your followers get notified.

</div>

- You need your [pass](../start/get-your-pass.md). You don't need to hold the coin.
- One open call per coin.
- You can post an update after a 25% move or 6 hours.

## How calls are judged

Your call's return is the market cap now against the market cap when you called. The peak is shown too. The **Top callers** board ranks people on all their calls, losses included, so a few lucky hits don't beat a steady record. Calls on your own coin don't count toward it.

## PnL card

Tap **PnL** on a coin's page to share your position. Up or down, the card shows your %, entry → now market cap, what you put in, what you've taken out and what you still hold. **Closed** if you've sold out. Numbers come from your on-chain trades, priced in dollars at the time of each trade, and are frozen when you share.

Share it: **Post on EdgeSocial**, **Share to X**, **Share to Telegram**, or **Save the image**.

## Profile stats

Your profile shows your trading across all three chains: trades, volume, realised and unrealised PnL, and your win rate (positions closed in profit, out of all closed positions). History is priced at trade time and never moves. After 10 trades across 3 coins you get your **Trader DNA**: grades for Overall, Entry, Exit, Conviction and Risk.

## Trophies for callers

*Said it out loud* for your first call, caller ladders at 5, 25 and 100 calls, and bullseyes for calls that did 2×, 10× and 50×. See [Trophy cabinet](../social/trophy-cabinet.md).

!!! warning "A call is an opinion"
    Calls and PnL cards are people's own posts. Not advice. Faking a call or a PnL card is against the rules and can be reported.
