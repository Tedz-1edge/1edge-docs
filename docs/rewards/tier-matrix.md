# The 1Edge Account Tier Matrix

Every 1Edge account sits on a five-rung tier ladder. Climbing it earns you two things: a higher **rebate on the trading fees you pay yourself**, and a higher **ecosystem point multiplier**. Your tier updates automatically on-chain as your volume and referral count grow.

## The matrix

| Tier | Volume threshold | Referral requirement | Fee rebate | Point multiplier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core** | Baseline | Verified human setup | **10%** | 1.0× |
| **Alpha** | 100 SOL | *or* 25 active referrals | **15%** | 1.5× |
| **Elite** | 500 SOL | *or* 250 active referrals | **20%** | 2.0× |
| **Apex** | 2,000 SOL | *or* 1,000 active referrals | **25%** | 3.0× |
| **Seed** *(Founding)* | — | First 50 wallets to hit 25 referrals | **30% for life** | 3.5× |

> ℹ️ **Two paths up.** You reach each tier by hitting **either** the volume threshold **or** the referral requirement — whichever you get to first. Volume-only traders and referral-only community builders both progress.

## What the rebate applies to

The tier rebate is a percentage of **the trading fees you pay yourself** — the higher your tier, the more of every trade you keep. This is distinct from the [referral fee-share](referral-engine.md), which pays you on fees generated by wallets you refer. The two stack.

> ✅ At **Apex**, you keep 25% of your own trading fees *and* earn 25% of every referred wallet's fees — on top of a 3× point multiplier.

## The tiers

### Core — the entry tier

Every verified human starts at Core. It is not a "free trial" tier — it already carries a **10% fee rebate** and full platform access from day one. Verifying your humanity is all it takes.

### Alpha · Elite · Apex — the climb

These three tiers are earned through activity. Each can be reached by trading volume **or** by building a referral network:

* **Alpha** — 100 SOL volume *or* 25 referrals → 15% rebate, 1.5× points
* **Elite** — 500 SOL volume *or* 250 referrals → 20% rebate, 2.0× points
* **Apex** — 2,000 SOL volume *or* 1,000 referrals → 25% rebate, 3.0× points

### Seed — the founding tier

**Seed is the rarest tier on 1Edge — only 50 will ever exist.** It is awarded to the **first 50 wallets to reach 25 referrals**, and it carries a **30% fee rebate for life**, a 3.5× point multiplier, and — for founding members — a future revenue share (details to be confirmed).

> ⚠️ **Limited to 50, forever.** Once all 50 Seed positions are claimed, the tier is closed permanently. There is no other way in.

## How tiers update

Tiers are tracked **on-chain** and update automatically as your verified volume and referral count grow. Rebates accrue continuously and can be claimed at any time — there is no manual tier-upgrade step.

## Point multipliers

Alongside fee rebates, each tier carries an **ecosystem point multiplier** (1.0× → 3.5×) applied to the points you earn from platform activity. Points feed into the broader 1Edge ecosystem and leaderboards. Higher tiers therefore compound in two directions at once: cheaper trading *and* faster point accumulation.
