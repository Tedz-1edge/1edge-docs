# The 1EDGE Account <span class="g">Tier Matrix</span>

Every 1EDGE account sits on a five-rung tier ladder. Climbing it earns you two things: a higher **rebate on the 1EDGE platform fee you pay on your own trades**, and a higher **ecosystem point multiplier**. A tier is a rate, not a payout: it sets how much of the fee you pay comes back to you.

You earn your tier **once, for your whole account**. Volume on every chain counts toward one ladder, measured in **US dollars at the time of each trade**, and a tier earned on one chain is offered on every chain you hold a pass on.

## The matrix

| Tier | Volume threshold | Referral requirement | Fee rebate | Point multiplier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core** | Baseline | Pass minted | **10%** | 1.0× |
| **Alpha** | $10,000 | *or* 25 active referrals | **15%** | 1.5× |
| **Elite** | $50,000 | *or* 250 active referrals | **20%** | 2.0× |
| **Apex** | $200,000 | *or* 1,000 active referrals | **25%** | 3.0× |
| **Seed** *(Founding)* | Referral only | First 50 wallets to hit 25 referrals | **30% for life** | 3.5× |
| **Anu** *(Founders)* | Founders only | Not earnable | **None** | Founders |
| **Future tier** | Locked | Not yet released | **TBA** | TBA |

> ℹ️ **Two paths up.** You reach each tier by hitting **either** the volume threshold **or** the referral requirement, whichever you get to first. Volume-only traders and referral-only community builders both progress.

> **One ladder, every chain.** The volume figures are US dollars, valued when each trade happened and summed across Solana, Robinhood Chain and Arc. A referee counts once, however many chains they trade on.

## What the rebate applies to

The tier rebate is a percentage of the **1EDGE platform fee** on your own trades, the platform fee is one slice of the total trade fee (0.55% on an Edge 1EDGE launch, 0.56% on an Edge [open launch](../protocol/open-launches.md)), and your tier rebates 10%–30% of *that slice* back to you. The higher your tier, the more of it you keep. This is distinct from the [referral fee-share](referral-engine.md), which pays you on fees generated by wallets you refer. The two stack.

**The rebate percentages are the same on every chain**, and each pays in the chain's coin: SOL on Solana, ETH on Robinhood Chain. A fee paid in USDC on a Solana open launch is valued at the SOL price when the trade happened, and its rebate is paid in SOL. The volume thresholds in the table above are US dollars. Your [1EDGE Proof of Humanity pass](../introduction/proof-of-humanity.md) carries your tier when you activate your Robinhood side, so you don't start again at Core.

> ✅ At **Apex**, you get back 25% of the platform fee on your own trades *and* earn 25% of the platform fee on every referred wallet's trades, on top of a 3× point multiplier. Both are paid in the chain's coin and claimed together.

## Stock trades

Trades on an [EDGEstocks](../protocol/edgestocks.md) launch are handled differently:

* **No rebate on stock trades.** Your tier rebate does not apply to the fees you pay on a stock launch.
* **Stock volume still lifts your tier.** It is valued in US dollars and counts toward the ladder like any other volume, and the higher rebate it earns applies to your non-stock trades.

## The tiers

### Core: the entry tier

Every account holding a pass starts at Core. It is not a "free trial" tier, it already carries a **10% fee rebate** and full platform access from day one. Minting the pass is all it takes: no application, no social account, no ID.

### Alpha · Elite · Apex: the climb

These three tiers are earned through activity. Each can be reached by trading volume **or** by building a referral network:

* **Alpha**, $10,000 volume *or* 25 referrals → 15% rebate, 1.5× points
* **Elite**, $50,000 volume *or* 250 referrals → 20% rebate, 2.0× points
* **Apex**, $200,000 volume *or* 1,000 referrals → 25% rebate, 3.0× points

### Seed: the founding tier

**Seed is the rarest tier on 1EDGE, only 50 will ever exist.** It is awarded to the **first 50 wallets to reach 25 referrals**, and it carries a **30% fee rebate for life**, a 3.5× point multiplier, and, for founding members, a future revenue share (details to be confirmed).

> ⚠️ **Limited to 50, forever.** Once all 50 Seed positions are claimed, the tier is closed permanently. There is no other way in.

### Anu: the founders' tier

**Anu is the founders' NFT**, held only by the 1EDGE founding team. It sits outside the earn ladder: it is **not something you climb to**, and it carries **no fee rebate and no rewards at all**. The founders deliberately take **zero** from the rebate and referral pools, so every bit of that value stays with the community earning it. Anu is simply an on-chain marker of the people who built 1EDGE.

> ℹ️ **Founders earn nothing.** Anu holders receive 0% rebate by design. It's a credential, not a payout.

### And beyond

A further tier sits above the ladder and **hasn't been released yet**. When it goes live, it'll be documented here.

## How tiers update

Your tier is worked out from your volume and referrals across every chain, and it only ever goes up. The tier itself lives **on-chain**, on your pass, one record per chain. When you earn a new one, your dashboard offers the upgrade on each chain you hold a pass on; writing it there takes one signature on that chain, because only your wallet can sign for your wallet, and costs nothing but the network fee. Rebates accrue continuously and can be claimed at any time.

## Point multipliers

Alongside fee rebates, each tier carries an **ecosystem point multiplier** (1.0× → 3.5×) applied to the points you earn from platform activity. Points feed into the broader 1EDGE ecosystem and leaderboards. Higher tiers therefore compound in two directions at once: cheaper trading *and* faster point accumulation.
