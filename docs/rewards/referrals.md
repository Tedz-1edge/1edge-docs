# Referrals

<p class="ed-lead">Bring people in. You get <strong>25% of the 1EDGE fee</strong> on every trade they make, for life, on the chain they trade on.</p>

## Your link

`1edge.app/r/<your-link>`

Pick it when you [claim your name](../start/profile.md): 3 to 24 characters, lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. Find it on your "you're in" card, your Dashboard and in **Settings → Referrals**. It unfurls on X and Telegram as your own card.

First pick is free. One change every 30 days.

## How it pays

- **25% of the 1EDGE share of the fee** on every trade your referrals make. It doesn't come out of their pocket or the creator's.
- **For life.** One level, no pyramids.
- **On every chain**, paid in that chain's coin, into the same balance as your [rebates](tiers.md#claiming).
- A referral starts paying once they've traded **0.5 SOL** (or the same on Robinhood Chain or Arc). Both of you need a pass.
- Not on trades in a coin you launched yourself.

## They count toward your tier

People you referred who've traded count toward your tier: **25** for Alpha, **250** for Elite, **1,000** for Apex. See [Tiers](tiers.md).

## Telegram groups

Run the 1EDGE bot in your group? `/ref <your-link>` puts your link on the bot's Buy buttons. See [Telegram bot](../social/telegram-bot.md).
