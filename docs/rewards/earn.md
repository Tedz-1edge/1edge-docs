# Earn on stocks

<p class="ed-lead">Add USDG to a Robinhood Stock Token pool and earn a share of every trade. You own the position; fees go straight to your wallet, and you can take it all out any time.</p>

## How it works

- Pick a stock and a pool. Earn lists Uniswap v4 pools of **Robinhood Stock Tokens** paired with **USDG**, on Robinhood Chain, from Robinhood's own on-chain registry. Only real pools with real depth.
- Deposit USDG. Two transactions: part of your USDG is swapped into the stock, then your position is added to the pool.
- **The position NFT is in your own wallet.** 1EDGE never holds your funds, your position or a stock.
- **1EDGE takes no fee on Earn.**

## Read this first

The Earn page says it plainly, and so do we:

- **The APR isn't promised.** It's a 24-hour snapshot of what the pool paid, not a forecast.
- **Out of range earns nothing.** If the price leaves your range, you stop earning until it comes back.
- **Impermanent loss.** When the stock moves, you can end up with less than if you'd just held.
- **Price jumps at the open.** Stocks can gap when the US market opens.
- **Not for US persons.**

## Where you are matters

Earn isn't available in every country, and the list is wider than the US. Where it isn't, you'll see *Earn isn't available where you are. It is Robinhood's Stock Tokens, and they aren't offered in your country.* See [Regions](../help/regions.md).
