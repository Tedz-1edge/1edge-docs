# Seasons

<p class="ed-lead">A season is one calendar month. Every Friday at 20:00 UTC the week settles; the finale closes the month. Score across six things, reach 150 points, and you're in the running for a paid seat that week.</p>

## How points work

Six things, up to **1,000 points a week**:

| | Weight | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| **Referrals** | 25% | Friends you brought in who traded this week |
| **Trading** | 25% | What you traded this week, real trading only |
| **Calls** | 15% | How your calls this week played out. Losses count |
| **Launches** | 15% | Coins you launched and the buyers they pulled in |
| **Social** | 10% | People who read your posts and calls |
| **Being here** | 10% | Days you were on 1EDGE, using it yourself (up to 7) |

Each one tops out at what the busiest 5% did that week, and **the first steps count the most**. You don't need to be a whale on one axis; a bit of everything goes a long way.

## 150 to qualify

**150 points** in a week is the line. A **paid seat** that week needs three things:

1. 150 points,
2. at least **1 SOL of net trading** (or the same on Robinhood Chain or Arc),
3. your pass, on your opt-in to the season.

Only weeks where you reached 150 count toward your season total.

## Being here

*Being here* is a bonus on top of everything else, **not points on its own**. It only adds once the other five have taken you to 150. Using 1EDGE without trading, posting or calling scores nothing.

It counts days you were actually on **1edge.app**, using it yourself. Trading from Axiom or another terminal [counts as trading](../trade/other-terminals.md), but not as being here.

## Seats

Up to **500 paid seats a week**, in four bands, each band sharing a quarter of the week's pot:

| Band | Seats |
|---|---|
| Top 2% | up to 10 |
| Next 8% | up to 40 |
| Next 20% | up to 100 |
| Next 40% | up to 350 |

About 7 in 10 people who qualify get paid. An empty seat rolls back.

**Seats are worth a dollar amount, paid in $1EDGE** at the average price over the 7 days before each Friday lock. The weekly pot is set from $1EDGE's market cap. The finale pays double. Nothing here is a promise about any price.

**Claim within 28 days.** The tokens go straight to your wallet, no gas. Unclaimed seats roll back.

## Fair play

- **One person, one wallet.** Volume is netted per coin over the week, so round trips cancel out. Wash patterns (buy, move off-book, sell from a second wallet) put a payout on **Pending checks**: it's held, not lost, and a person decides.
- Every paid seat waits one more week before it's paid, so trading can be re-checked.
- 1EDGE team wallets don't score.

## The finale

When the month closes, the champion is presented **the season trophy**: dated, one of one, kept forever. The top three wear their crowns on their name through the next season. Everyone else sees their place and how far the next band was.

## Nudges

If you're in, you may get a bell when a rival passes you, when you're close to the next band, or before the Friday lock. At most 3 a day, with quiet hours.
