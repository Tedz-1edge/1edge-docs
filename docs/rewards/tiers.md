# Tiers and rebates

<p class="ed-lead">Part of the 1EDGE fee on every trade you make comes back to you. How much depends on your pass tier. Your tier only goes up.</p>

## The ladder

| Tier | Rebate | How you get there |
|---|---|---|
| **Core** | 10% | Your pass. From day one. |
| **Alpha** | 15% | **$10,000** traded **or** 25 referrals |
| **Elite** | 20% | **$50,000** traded **or** 250 referrals |
| **Apex** | 25% | **$200,000** traded **or** 1,000 referrals |

- **Whichever lands first.** Volume or referrals.
- **Volume is in dollars at the time of each trade, across every chain.** SOL, ETH and USDC trades all add up.
- **Referrals** count people you brought in who've traded (see [Referrals](referrals.md)).
- **One tier per person**, on every chain. Up only, never down.

**Seed** (30%) went to the earliest members and is closed. Some tiers are granted by 1EDGE, not earned.

## What the rebate is

A share of **the 1EDGE part** of the fee you pay, on your own trades. On an EDGE coin on the curve that's a share of the 0.55% house fee; on Tek, of the 1% house fee. After graduation on Meteora, it's a share of what 1EDGE actually keeps. It never costs the creator or the pool anything.

The rebate is the only thing a tier changes.

## Upgrading your pass

When you cross a line, your Dashboard shows **⬆ Upgrade to ALPHA — earned**. Sign once. It's free bar the tiny network fee, and your pass evolves in place. Your rebate rises the moment you cross, on every chain.

## Claiming

*Rebates and referral earnings share one balance on each chain, so one claim takes both. Each claim is sent from that chain's wallet and paid in its own currency. Nothing is converted or bridged.*

Claim from your Dashboard. Minimum claim on Solana: 0.005 SOL.
